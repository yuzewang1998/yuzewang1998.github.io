Yuze Wang | 王宇泽

I'm a Ph.D. student under the supervision of Prof. Yue Qi at the State Key Laboratory of Virtual Reality Technology and Systems, School of Computer Science and Engineering, Beihang University.

My research combines Computer Vision (CV), Computer Graphics (CG), and Extended Reality (XR) techniques to enable low-cost, interactive 3D scene generation from web-sourced multimodal data, with the long-term goal of powering immersive XR tourism experiences.

Feel free to contact me for any questions, collaboration opportunities, or just for a chat.
